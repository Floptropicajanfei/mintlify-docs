With Chronos, we also offer the customization and creation of your own theme for your board(s)

# How to create a Custom Theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate a pre-made theme in one of the boards">
    Duplicate it and do not move it from the Themes frame, this will cause the board to stop working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    To begin with your Custom Theme creation, edit the name of the frame, you can make it into whatever you want (Do not use the word Themes, or any other name of a frame in Chronos)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the ImageLabel">
    You can edit the image label by entering your own decal ID, make sure it is 1536 x 1024px to fit into the screen fully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the Colors">
    You can choose any colour that you want, there is no borders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="EventTemplate & ShiftTrainingTemplate">
    In this, you can customize the location of certain textlabels, images and etc, you can also customize the UI Corners and add gradients if you wish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Topbar">
    With the topbar, you are not allowed to remove the Chronos logo, if you do remove it the board will break. Feel free to customize everything else, editing any texts will result in it being automatically edited by the scripts and your configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Thats it!">
    <Danger>
      Do not rename, move or rearrange anything or the boards and system will fully break until it is correctly fixed.
    </Danger>
  </Step>
</Steps>

At this moment in time, we do not offer support for any custom features. Tickets which are regarding support with Custom features or helping solve techincal issues will result in the ticket being closed and you being redirected to the Documentation page.
